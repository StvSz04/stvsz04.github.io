Week 4 June 22 - 28

Goals

  • Do practice presentation of demo video slides and script during meeting, implement feedback
  • Film demo video and upload on Youtube for demo paper
  • Work on final revisions of the demo paper for submission to SPLASH/ISSTA 2026

Approach and implementation

During our weekly meeting, I did a practice presentation using the demo video slides and script. After completing the presentation I recieved a lot of contructive feeback aimed to improve the overall quality of the presentation. This was a great learning experience as it was my first time making a professional presentation.

Next on Wednesday, I spent around 9 hours filming, editing, and uploading the demo video. This process was challenging but very rewarding and benefical to my growth as a professional. The goal of the demo video was to present the accompanying slide deck and also an example usage of the DM# demo tool. For the example usage, I used a FCNN trained on the MNIST dataset to illustrate the the complete workflow of DM#, including highlighting is acceleration capabilities within DNN mutation analysis.

Before our submission deadline of 6/26 for SPLASH/ISSTA 2026, my lab partners and I had to make final revisions to the paper. My mentor recommened that we revist the orginial paper of DM# to make sure that we had a solid understanding of the inner workings of the tool. Next, he advised us to read over the demo paper and focus on rewriting exiting sections to have more clarity and academic tone. Using this direction I revised multiple sections of the paper, overall improving the quality.

Results

  • Presented demo video slide deck and script during meeting, and implemented feeback into slides and script
  • Filmed demo video and uploaded to Youtube for demo paper
  • Finalised revisions and submitted demo paper for SPLASH/ISSTA 2026
